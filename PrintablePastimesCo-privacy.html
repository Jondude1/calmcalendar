Privacy Policy

PrintablePastimesCo Pinterest Publisher · Last updated October 12, 2026

What this app does

This app publishes pins (images, titles, descriptions, and destination links) to our own Pinterest business account through the official Pinterest API. It is used solely by PrintablePastimesCo to share our own products. It is for our own use only.

Data we collect

We do not collect personal data from anyone. The app stores Pinterest OAuth tokens securely and uses them only to create pins on our own account. It does not access any other user's account or data.

Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties.
